Our story

Two decades of practice

Firas I. Korban Ali & Co. is an Iraqi professional firm providing assurance, tax, advisory, and training services, founded by two certified public accountants, Dr. Firas Korban Ali and Dr. Adnan Al-Qadhi.

Though the firm was established in 2017, Dr. Firas has practiced since 2003 — giving the company a foundation of more than twenty years of continuous professional experience.

We understand the responsibility of presenting a true and fair view of a company’s position. We work alongside you in the Iraqi market to build shareholder trust, support informed decisions, and shape a more confident future.
